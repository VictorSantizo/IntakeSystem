Fill blanks on harvested records

Records imported from the manual filing cabinet often have blank amounts or dates. This re-reads each document and fills only the blank fields — existing values are never touched, and nothing on disk is moved or renamed. Costs about one cent per record.

Retro-attach source emails

Documents ingested before July 7 never had their source email stored, so their record pages can’t show or print the email. This re-reads the office mailboxes and matches emails to those documents by exact file content — never guesswork. Mailboxes are only ever read: nothing is marked as read, moved, or deleted (IMAP peek / POP3 TOP only). Results land in Ops Reports as “Email backfill”.
