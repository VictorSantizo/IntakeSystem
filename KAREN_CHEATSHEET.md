# Klickity Klack — Quick Guide for Karen

A friendly cheat sheet for the everyday tasks. Nothing here can break anything —
click around and explore.

---

## Getting in
- Open your browser to **http://192.168.10.240:5050** (bookmark it).
- Sign in with your name and password. The **👤 your name** in the top-right is you;
  **Sign out** is next to it.
- The top menu bar is always there: **Dashboard · Review Queue · Intake Log · Bills ·
  Catch Up · + Manual Entry** (and **⚙ Settings** if you're an admin).

---

## The five main areas
| Where | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | The big picture — totals and recent activity. Click any number to jump to the list behind it. |
| **Review Queue** | New scans/emails waiting for you to check and confirm. This is your inbox. |
| **Intake Log** | Every record you've confirmed. Search and filter here. |
| **Bills** | What's owed, by vendor and by due date. |
| **Catch Up** | Overdue / behind items to work through. |

## Scanning the mail (with a cover sheet)
1. On the **Review Queue** page, click **★ Print Cover Sheet**.
2. Write the **date the mail was received** in the big boxes, put it on **top** of the stack,
   and scan the whole stack.
3. Everything in that scan gets grouped into one **batch** and stamped with that received date.
   *(No cover sheet? It still works — it just assumes today's date, which you can fix later.)*
> Tip: the received date matters — it's what decides which bills get paid first.

## Reviewing & confirming documents
1. Go to **Review Queue**. Each card is one document, with what the computer *thinks* it is.
2. Click **Review & Confirm →** to open it: the **document is on the left, the fields on the
   right**. Fix anything that's wrong.
3. Click **Confirm** (or press **Ctrl + Enter**) to file it. You'll move straight to the next one.
4. **In a hurry?** Tick the checkboxes on several cards and click **✓ Confirm Selected**, or
   **Select all high-confidence** to grab the easy ones at once.

**Filters at the top of the queue:** narrow by **Batch, Received date, Type, Entity, Bill type,
Confidence,** or **Search**. Whatever you filter to, the fast-lane and Prev/Next stay inside
that group.

## Printing documents on paper
- Tick the documents you want, then click **🖨️ Print Selected**. They're merged into **one PDF**
  that opens in a new tab — just print that.

---

## Marking a bill paid
Two easy ways:
- **From the record:** open it and click the green **✓ Mark Paid**. A little form drops down —
  fill in **Paid Date, Amount, Method** (Check / ACH / Credit Card / Phone). Choose **Check** and
  a **Check #** box appears. Add a **Note** if you like. Click **Save Payment**.
- **A whole vendor at once:** on a vendor's page, **✓ Mark All as Paid**.

When you mark something paid by check, the filed document is automatically renamed to include
the **CK# number**, just like your old filing.

## Finding a record
- **Intake Log** → use **Search** (vendor, property, notes) or the dropdowns
  (Entity, Property, Doc Type, Status, date range), then **Filter**.
- **Click any row** to open the full record (document left, fields right). This works on the
  Log, the Bills lists, the Dashboard activity, and the vendor pages.

## Moving around a record
- Inside a record you'll see **← Back to [list]**, **← Prev**, and **Next →** — these walk
  through the exact list you came from.
- When you **Edit** and **Save**, you now **stay on that record** (no more hunting for it in the
  list afterward).

## Viewing a document
- Any **Open / View** button shows the PDF **right inside the app** in a pop-up viewer.
  Press **Esc** or click outside it to close. (No more getting lost in new browser tabs.)

## Handy extras
- **New bill type on the fly:** in the review screen's **Bill Type** box, start typing. If it's
  not on the list, click **“+ Add … as a new bill type.”** No trip to Settings.
- **Email bills:** for a bill that came by email, the record shows a **📧 Source Email** panel —
  click **View full email** to read who sent it, the subject, date, and the message.
- **Receipts vs bills:** a *receipt* (already paid) is now its own type, separate from a *bill*
  (still owed).
- **Something looks wrong?** Click **🐛 Report a problem** in the top bar — it sends us the page
  and details automatically.
- **Change the look:** the theme picker (top-right) has Classic / Hippie / Rock Star / Coastal.
  It only changes *your* view.
